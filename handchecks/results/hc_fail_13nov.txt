HC-FAIL, the real case: 13 November 2026, when GitHub skipped the schedule.

The rest of HC-FAIL breaks things on purpose. This is the one that happened.

What the workflow asks for
--------------------------
`.github/workflows/forecast.yml` has two cron lines:

    17,47 6-11 * * *      twelve attempts, 06:17 to 11:47 UTC
    23 12-22 * * *        eleven more, 12:23 to 22:23 UTC

Twenty-three attempts a day. Every one after the first finds the run already
built and does nothing, which is why there can be so many.

What actually ran
-----------------
`gh run list --workflow forecast.yml`, read on 13 November 2026 at 12:31
UTC:

    2026-11-13T07:02:32Z  workflow_dispatch  success   43382867764
    2026-11-12T17:44:34Z  schedule           success   43272620895
    2026-11-12T10:55:47Z  workflow_dispatch  success   43204664470
    2026-11-12T10:20:30Z  workflow_dispatch  success   43199493835
    2026-11-12T09:13:05Z  workflow_dispatch  success   43189501341
    2026-11-12T09:08:25Z  workflow_dispatch  success   43188801507

On 12 November one of the twenty-three fired, at 17:44 UTC, twenty-one
minutes after the 17:23 it was asked for. Its log reads

    already published: 20261112T00Z
    decision: 20261112T00Z has already been built

which is right: the 13 November run did not exist yet.

**On 13 November not one scheduled attempt fired.** By 12:31 UTC thirteen of
them were due -- 06:17, 06:47, 07:17, 07:47, 08:17, 08:47, 09:17, 09:47,
10:17, 10:47, 11:17, 11:47 and 12:23 -- and the run list has none of them.
The only forecast run of the day is the manual dispatch at 07:02 UTC.

What the site did in the meantime
---------------------------------
It kept the 12 November run, which is what SPEC 1.5 asks for. Nothing was
half-published, nothing was blank and no page said anything it could not
stand behind; it simply showed an older forecast, with the run and its time
in the run line as always.

The manual dispatch at 07:02 UTC built and published 20261113T00Z:

    12:33:31 IST  20261113T00Z: built. day 1 highest count at 10 mm over
                  India = 37 of 51
    12:35:21 IST  20261113T00Z: published 60 files, 3.45 MB, 0 old blob(s)
                  deleted

That was 31.5 hours after the 12 November run time, inside the 36 hours of
SPEC 11.8, so no banner was ever shown to a visitor. Had the dispatch not
been made, the banner in `hc_fail_stale.jpg` is what the site would have
shown from 12:00 UTC today onwards -- which is exactly how that screenshot
was taken, from the same run, once it really had passed 36 hours.

What this says
--------------
GitHub does not promise to run a scheduled workflow at the time asked for,
or at all, and on this repository it has now missed 22 of 23 attempts on one
day and 23 of 23 on the next. The schedule is not something the published
track record can lean on. The site's own defence is the one measured here:
an old run stays, says how old it is past 36 hours, and is never replaced by
a broken one.
